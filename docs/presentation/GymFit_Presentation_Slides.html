The scientifically validated formula to estimate Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) at rest:

Formula Definition

For Males:
BMR = 10W + 6.25H - 5A + 5

For Females:
BMR = 10W + 6.25H - 5A - 161

Where W = Weight (kg), H = Height (cm), A = Age (years).

Benchmark Verification (Your Test Case)

Male Athlete: 80 kg, 175 cm, 24 years
BMR = 10(80) + 6.25(175) - 5(24) + 5
BMR = 800 + 1093.75 - 120 + 5
BMR = 1778.75 kcal/day ≈ 1779 kcal

Smart Substitution Algorithm

Enables members to swap foods (e.g. Dairy to Vegan, or Chicken to Paneer) while dynamically preserving the target calorie and protein profile.

Macro-Distance Penalty:
Score = 1.0 × |ΔCalories| + 4.0 × |ΔProtein|

Higher penalty weight (4.0) assigned to protein variance to ensure hypertrophy & retention targets remain intact.

Example: Swapping 100g Raw Paneer

Alternative Calories Protein Score
Paneer (Current) 265 kcal 18g 0.0
Firm Tofu (150g) 216 kcal 18g 49.0
Soya Chunks (50g) 172 kcal 26g 125.0

Respects allergies (Lactose, Gluten, Soy, Nuts) and automatically updates total daily metrics.

Incline Dumbbell Press Biomechanics

  • Anatomical Focus: Clavicular head of Pectoralis Major, Anterior Deltoids, and Triceps Brachii.
  • Bench Calibration: Set strictly at $30^\circ\text{ to }45^\circ$ to minimize anterior deltoid impingement.
  • Elbow Flaring Prevention: Cued to maintain a $45^\circ\text{ to }60^\circ$ elbow tuck relative to the torso.
  • Scapular Position: Full retraction and depression against the pad throughout both eccentric and concentric phases.
