[WHAT TO SAY OUT LOUD]:
"This is our diet onboarding wizard:

Instead of guessing calories or using generic charts, we implemented the 
clinically validated Mifflin–St Jeor equation:
- For males: BMR = 10W + 6.25H - 5A + 5
- For females: BMR = 10W + 6.25H - 5A - 161
Where W is weight in kg, H is height in cm, and A is age in years.

The user selects their activity multiplier from Sedentary (1.2x) to Extremely

FREQUENTLY ASKED PANEL QUESTIONS & WINNING ANSWERS
================================================================================

Q1: "Why did you choose Three.js for the 3D model?"
A1: "Three.js is a lightweight WebGL library that runs natively in modern web 
     browsers without requiring users to install any external plugins, unity 
     players, or heavy software."

Q2: "How does the raycasting work when clicking on a muscle?"
A2: "When the user clicks, Three.js casts a 3D mathematical ray from the 2D 
     screen coordinate into the 3D world space. The first polygon mesh 
     intersected by that ray is detected, and its name is mapped to our exercise 
     database."

Q3: "Why use the Mifflin–St Jeor formula instead of Harris-Benedict?"
A3: "Mifflin–St Jeor was established in 1990 and is recognized by the American 
     Dietetic Association as the gold standard with an error margin under 10%, 
     whereas the older Harris-Benedict formula tends to overestimate energy 
     needs by 5% to 15%."

Q4: "Why did you arrange the diet into 3 boxes instead of traditional meal lists?"
A4: "Traditional apps force users to scroll through 1200+ pixels vertically to 
     inspect 5 to 7 meals. Our 3-box design groups morning, mid-day, and evening 
     phases horizontally, allowing the user to view their complete daily 
     nutrition on one screen with zero scroll fatigue."

Q5: "Where is the data stored right now?"
A5: "Currently, in our working prototype, the diet plan and user metrics are 
     persisted in browser localStorage. This allows instant loading and offline 
     functionality. In our architecture roadmap, this connects to a Spring Boot 
     REST API with MySQL."
