Motivation
Problem Statement & Market Deficiencies
Industry Gaps

1. Flaws in Conventional Fitness Apps

  • 65%+ Beginner Drop-out: Members drop memberships within 90 days due to lack of customized diet and form guidance.
  • Unscientific Caloric Targets: Standard apps assign arbitrary caloric numbers without evaluating resting metabolic rate.
  • Severe Vertical Scroll Fatigue: Viewing a 5-meal daily plan requires scrolling through 1200px+ of UI clutter, leading to logging abandonment.
  • Disorganized Workouts: Static text instructions fail to communicate 3D bar paths and joint angle safety.

2. The GymFit Engineering Solution

  • Clinical Precision: Formulates exact daily requirements using the Mifflin–St Jeor BMR equation and TDEE scalars.
  • Single-Screen 3-Box Nutrition: Fits all daily meals (Breakfast, Lunch/Pre-Workout, Dinner/Post-Workout) onto one screen.
  • Dynamic Food Replacement: Real-time substitution engine preserving target calories and protein.
  • Autonomous Operation: Focuses directly on member self-reliance and administrative oversight with no trainer dependency.

Metabolism
Mifflin–St Jeor Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) Formulation
Nutritional Mathematics

Clinical Mathematical Formulation

The Mifflin–St Jeor equation estimates calories required at rest to maintain essential autonomic life functions:

For Males:
BMR = 10W + 6.25H - 5A + 5

For Females:
BMR = 10W + 6.25H - 5A - 161

Where: W = Weight (kg), H = Height (cm), A = Age (years).

Benchmark Verification (Your Exact Test Case)

Input parameters: Male athlete, W = 80 kg, H = 175 cm, Age = 24 years:

BMR = 10(80) + 6.25(175) - 5(24) + 5
BMR = 800 + 1093.75 - 120 + 5
BMR = 1778.75 kcal/day ≈ 1779 kcal/day

✔ Implemented into nutritionCalculator.js with exact double precision.

Algorithms
Smart Food Replacement & Allergen Engine
Nutritional Engine

Macro-Distance Penalty Function

When replacing an ingredient (e.g. Dairy to Vegan), candidate foods are evaluated using a weighted penalty score:

Penalty Score = 1.0 × |ΔCalories| + 4.0 × |ΔProtein|
  • Protein Retention Weight: Quadrupled factor ($\beta = 4.0$) prioritizes maintaining muscle hypertrophy targets.
  • Allergen Safety Checks: Filtered against declared user intolerances (Lactose, Gluten, Soy, Peanuts).
  • Dynamic Recalculation: Updates meal and day metrics in real time.

Example: Replacing 100g Raw Paneer

Food Item Calories Protein Score
Raw Paneer (Baseline) 265 kcal 18g 0.0 (Base)
Firm Tofu (150g) 216 kcal 18g 49.0 (Best)
Soya Chunks (50g) 172 kcal 26g 125.0

Preserves the macro profile while seamlessly accommodating vegan, vegetarian, or lactose-intolerant preferences.

3D Exercise Biomechanics & Workout Engine
Exercise Execution

Incline Dumbbell Press Biomechanics

  • Primary Target: Clavicular head of Pectoralis Major (Upper Chest).
  • Synergists: Anterior Deltoid & Triceps Brachii.
  • Bench Angle Optimization: Calibrated at $30^\circ\text{–}45^\circ$ to emphasize upper chest fibers while mitigating anterior deltoid impingement.
  • Safety Mechanics: Cues $45^\circ\text{–}60^\circ$ elbow tuck relative to torso, preventing shoulder labrum wear.

Session Tracking & Progressive Overload

  • Dynamic Set Logging: Records load (kg), repetitions, and Rate of Perceived Exertion (RPE).
  • Rest Timer Integration: Automated chronometer optimizing phosphocreatine replenishment between heavy compound sets.
  • Volume Progression Charts: Tracks cumulative weekly tonnage across muscle groups.
