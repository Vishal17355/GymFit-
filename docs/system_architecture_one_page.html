Stateless Bearer JWT tokens, role guards (member / admin), sanitized payloads, CORS protection.

Mifflin–St Jeor BMR Engine

Calculates resting energy using exact clinical equations for males (+5) and females (-161).

TDEE & Goal Calibration

Activity scalars ($1.200\text{–}1.900\times$).
Bulking (+350 kcal), Cutting (-400 kcal), Maintenance (+0 kcal).

Smart Food Swap Engine

Euclidean macro penalty function ranking allergen-safe substitutes (4x protein weight).

3. Data & Persistence Tier Schema Models • Persistent Store

7-Day schedules, target calories & macros, meal slot items, dynamic eaten status flags.

Facilities & Logs

Membership subscription tiers, revenue logs, gym machinery service histories.

Mifflin–St Jeor Energy Equations
Male BMR = 10W + 6.25H - 5A + 5
Female BMR = 10W + 6.25H - 5A - 161
TDEE = BMR × [1.20 | 1.375 | 1.55 | 1.725 | 1.90]
Gym Macro Partitioning & Swap Formula
Protein = 1.4 to 2.0 g/kg (4 kcal/g)
Fat = 25% Total Calories (9 kcal/g)
Carbs = [TargetCal - (ProtCal + FatCal)] / 4
Swap Penalty = 1.0·|ΔCalories| + 4.0·|ΔProtein|
